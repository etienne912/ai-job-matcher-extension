Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

AI Job Matcher ("the extension") is a Chrome browser extension that analyses job listings against your CV and preferences using an AI provider of your choice. This policy explains what data the extension handles and how.

Data collected and stored locally

The following information is stored exclusively in your browser using chrome.storage.local, which is protected by your Chrome profile and never leaves your device unless you explicitly trigger an analysis:

  • Your CV text
  • Job preferences (target titles, salary, work arrangement, skills, deal-breakers)
  • Your chosen AI provider and model
  • Your AI provider API key (if using a cloud provider)

Data sent to third parties

When you analyse a job listing, the following is sent to the AI provider you have selected in settings:

  • The text content of the job listing page
  • Your CV and job preferences (as part of the analysis prompt)
  • Your API key (to authenticate with the provider)

The extension supports the following providers. Each has its own privacy policy:

  • Anthropic (Claude) — anthropic.com/privacy
  • OpenAI — openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
  • Google Gemini — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Mistral AI — mistral.ai/privacy
  • Ollama (local) — All processing happens on your own machine. No data is sent over the network.

Data not collected

The extension does not:

  • Collect analytics or usage data
  • Send any data to the extension developer
  • Track your browsing history
  • Share your information with any party other than your chosen AI provider
  • Store data on any remote server

Permissions

The extension requests the following Chrome permissions:

  • storage — to save your CV and preferences locally
  • activeTab — to read the current page when a job listing is detected
  • sidePanel — to display the analysis panel alongside the page
  • Host access (all URLs) — job listings appear on any website (company career pages, job boards, LinkedIn, etc.), so the extension needs permission to detect them wherever they appear
